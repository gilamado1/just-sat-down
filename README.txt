JUST SAT DOWN — three games
===========================
Open index.html (or any of the three) in a phone or desktop browser.

1) sit.html  — Just Sat Down
   You start on the couch. She calls. One request.
   Tap the yellow button. HOLD when it says HOLD. MASH when it says MASH.
   Trap questions have no good answer. That is the joke.
   3 sigh strikes and you need to talk.

2) prep.html — Prep the House
   Tap the glowing problems before the clock hits zero.
   The rumor ticker tells you which disasters are coming.

3) dash.html — Dash to the Couch
   Drag him (or WASD). Dodge Barnaby. Do not meet her eyes.
   Touch the couch to sit down.

They used to be one game. That was a mistake.
