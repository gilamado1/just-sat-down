JUST SAT DOWN™ — A Marriage Simulator
====================================
A one-thumb arcade marriage simulator.

The minute you sit on the couch, she asks for something.

HOW TO PLAY
- PREP (15s): tap the glowing hotspots to stop requests before they happen.
  Read the rumor ticker — it hints what's coming today.
- DASH: drag (or WASD) the husband to the couch. Dodge Barnaby the dog,
  the kid, the laundry basket, and HER GAZE.
- RELAX: defend your peace. Every request is a tiny physical game:
  swipe the phone, swat the spider, stop the thermostat needle in green,
  hold the lamp steady, survive "Do I look tired?" by saying nothing,
  rapid-tap the cushions, intercept Barnaby's shoe heist,
  pick a bag of chips (both answers are a crime), stop the nose in the zone.
- The SIGH meter fills when things go wrong. 100 = a strike.
  3 strikes = "WE NEED TO TALK." (45 minutes, no skip button.)
- Earn PP, spend it in the Couch Emporium: Cup Holder, Remote Holster,
  Smart Thermostat, Bug Zapper, Couch Ejector Seat.

RUN IT
- Just open index.html in any modern browser (desktop or phone).
- Or: python3 -m http.server 8321  →  http://localhost:8321
